About Ciret

Specialist solutions for painting & decorating

Welcome to Ciret Ltd, manufacturers and suppliers of quality painting and decorating products. We provide expert solutions to the trade and retail industry.

Our wide range helps contractors and DIY enthusiasts finish decorating projects efficiently and cost-effectively. Paint is the only product we don’t offer. Everything around it, we do.
